I am a student at EFREI Paris and I wanted a subject where the number we hear in the news and what happens on the ground can be different.

My interest in renewable energy is also connected to my personal experience. I grew up in a rural village where access to electricity was limited. Before the arrival of the electrical grid, solar panels played an important role in providing basic electricity for everyday life. Later, as an Electronics Technician, I participated in the maintenance and management of solar installations. This experience gave me a practical understanding of both the importance and the challenges of sustainable energy systems.

The energy transition is exactly the kind of topic where national statistics can hide important local realities. We often hear a single national figure, but electricity is produced region by region, and the regions do not start from the same situation.

The data used in this project is public, monthly and regional, allowing me to explore these differences directly rather than simply repeating national trends. That is why this dashboard focuses on regional progress and not only on national totals.
