# Demo video script (about 3 min 30)

Record the deployed app in a wide window (1440 px or more), in 1080p, with the filters reset. I speak in English because the app is in English. Short sentences, and I let each chart stay on screen for two seconds before I talk about it.

| Time | On screen | What I say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:25 | Header, KPIs, tab 1 | "I am Soumahoro Souleymane, a student at EFREI. In the news we hear one national number about renewable electricity. I wanted to see what happens region by region. So my question is: are all French regions moving toward a renewable electricity future, and at the same speed? I used the monthly regional production published by RTE on data.gouv.fr." |
| 0:25 to 0:55 | Tab 2, stacked bars, hover on 2014 and 2025 | "Renewable production went up by about 60 % between 2014 and 2025. Look at the colours: wind and solar did most of the work. Hydro, in dark blue, stays flat because it follows the rain." |
| 0:55 to 1:15 | Tab 2, what replaced what | "What did the renewables replace? Mostly nuclear production, not fossil fuel, which was already small. Solar grows fastest in rate and wind adds the most volume, so I always show both." |
| 1:15 to 1:45 | Tab 3, map, then switch the sidebar indicator from level to progress | "Now the regions. By level, the leaders are regions without a nuclear plant, like Bourgogne-Franche-Comté. If I switch to progress, the map changes: Pays de la Loire gained more than fifty points, then Île-de-France and Hauts-de-France. Level and progress do not tell the same story." |
| 1:45 to 2:10 | Quadrant, profiles, region in focus | "This chart crosses the two measures and gives four profiles. Regions that started low do not progress faster, there is no systematic catch-up. And here I can pick any region and see its mix and its rank over the years." |
| 2:10 to 2:30 | Tab 4, seasonal charts | "Renewable electricity also has a season. Solar peaks in summer, wind in winter, and their seasonal shapes are almost opposite, so they complement each other." |
| 2:30 to 2:50 | Tab 4, gaps | "Are the gaps between regions closing? No. The dispersion does not shrink, and it grows for wind, solar and bioenergy. The divide between regions with and without nuclear is a large part of it." |
| 2:50 to 3:10 | Tab 5 | "In the what-if tab I can change the weights of my transition score and the ranking stays stable. I can also test a scenario where wind and solar keep growing, but it is only arithmetic with written assumptions, not a forecast." |
| 3:10 to 3:30 | Tab 7, then tab 6 | "To finish, the data quality tab: the checks, the nuclear zeros that are structural, the negatives and extremes I flagged, the licence. My conclusion is to compare regions on progress and not only on level, to look at the sources, and to treat the gaps between regions as a policy question. Thank you." |

Before recording: reset the filters, close other tabs, zoom at 100 %, run through the clicks once, keep the final video under four minutes, then paste the link in `README.md`.
